## Week 1

### VPC

A Virtual Private Server gives you a private , isolated network boundary. Your own address space. Your own routing rules, your own firewall. so your infrastructure behaves like it's sitting in a private data center, even though it's running on AWS's shared hardware.

### Avaliability Zone

An avaliability zone consists of one or more data centers with redundant powerr network and connectivity.

### Region

Group of Avaliability Zones

### AWS IAM

Authentication is verifying if someone who they say they are. Authorization is do you have the correct permissions.
